Museum Studies
Advanced Technical Certificate | Code: C6050 | 16 credits
CIP (0650010266)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)


The Advanced Technical Certificate (ATC) in Museum Studies offers a dynamic and interdisciplinary education that prepares
students for impactful careers at the intersection of museum work and interactive media production. This innovative program
equips learners with critical skills enabling them to craft engaging traditional and interactive experiences. Simultaneously,
students will explore the field of museum studies, gaining in-depth training in the analysis, interpretation, and curation of
museum artifacts, while developing a profound appreciation for their historical and cultural contexts. Through hands-on learning
opportunities, participants will apply ethical standards and professional practices in the responsible management and
preservation of collections. With a strong focus on research, documentation, and exhibition design, graduates will emerge as
versatile professionals prepared to navigate the evolving media and museum landscapes, effectively engaging various audiences
and fostering a deeper understanding of cultural heritage and storytelling across multiple platforms.
