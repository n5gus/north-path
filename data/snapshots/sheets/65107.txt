Sales and Customer Management
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65107 | 12 Credits
CIP: (0252180400)
Effective Term: Fall 2023 (2237)

The certificate in Sales and Customer Management is designed to enhance knowledge and practical skills for a
successful career in the sales profession. Students will be able to use customer relationship management (CRM) and
selling techniques for reaching target markets to sell a product or service to sustain profitability and competitiveness.

MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (12.00 Credits)

 MKA 1160         Customer Relationship Management                                   (3 credits)
 MAR 1502         Sales and Consumer Behavior                                        (3 credits)
 MKA 1022         Relationship Selling                                               (3 credits)
 MAR 1440         Fundamentals of Negotiations                                       (3 credits)
