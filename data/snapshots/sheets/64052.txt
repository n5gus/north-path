Translation and Interpretation
College Credit Certificate | Code: 64052 | 18 Credits
CIP: (0713100306)
Effective Date: Fall 2021 (2217)


This College Credit Certificate (CCC) focuses on the basic preparation needed for a career in Translation and Interpretation.
Students learn how language functions and apply this knowledge to the development of Sight and Consecutive Interpretation
skills. The development of basic translation competencies is also covered with emphasis on the use of Computer Assisted
Translation software.

MAJOR CORE REQUIREMENTS (18.00 CREDITS)
FOT 2802       Introduction to Translation                        (3 Credits)
FOT 2821       Introduction to Interpretation                     (3 Credits)
FOT 2823       Consecutive Interpretation                         (3 Credits)        Prerequisite: FOT 2821
FOT 2825       Computer Assisted Translation                      (3 Credits)        Prerequisite: FOT 2802
LIN 2011       Introduction to Linguistics                        (3 Credits)
SPT 2842       Contrastive Analysis Spanish/English               (3 Credits)
