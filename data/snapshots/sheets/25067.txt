Social and Human Services - Addictions Studies
Associate in Science | Code: 25067 | 60 credits
CIP (1451159901)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Social and Human Services program with a specialization in Addiction Studies is designed to prepare students for
employment as human services specialists, human services practitioners, chemical dependency practitioners, addiction
specialists, mental health and social service practitioners, or to provide supplemental training for persons previously or currently
employed in these occupations. The program is also designed to provide most of the general academic and addiction specific
requirements ofthe Certification Board for Addiction Professionals of Florida.
