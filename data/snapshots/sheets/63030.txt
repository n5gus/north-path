Endoscopic Technician
College Credit Certificate | Code: 63030 |24 Credits
CIP (0351099902)
Effective Term: Summer 2023 (2235)

The College Credit Certificate in Endoscopy Technician is designed to educate and prepare graduates with skills required for entry-
level employment as a member of the healthcare team. In addition, students will acquire extensive knowledge of legal and ethical
responsibilities, anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, microbiology, aseptic techniques, patient care procedures, patient safety,
and use and care of equipment and supplies.
