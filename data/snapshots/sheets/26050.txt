Graphic Internet Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 26050 | 60 credits
CIP (1611080103)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Graphic Internet Technology program is designed to prepare creative students for a rewarding and challenging career as
web designers. Students will develop a wide variety of Internet communications skills and will learn to design, produce and
distribute communications on the web.
