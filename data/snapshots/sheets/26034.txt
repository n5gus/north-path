Architectural Design and Construction Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 26034 | 66 credits
CIP (1604090100)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Architectural Design and Construction Technology program offers courses that enable the student to translate the design
and systems of the architect into graphic and written form and assists the professional in rendering architectural services. The
attainment of these skills qualifies the student for several specialties, such as, architectural drafting, cost estimating, material
selecting, specification writing and preparing presentations, drawings & models.
