Transportation and Logistics
Associate in Science | Code: 28000 | 64 credits
CIP (1652020301)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

This Transportation and Logistics A.S. degree program provides core courses in transportation policy, law, safety, security,
management and marketing, and an integrated understanding of the intermodal relationship between the various modes of
transportation. In addition, college credit certificates will be offered in Intermodal Freight Transportation and International Freight
Transportation.
