Early Childhood Education - Early Childhood Education Inclusion Specialization
College Credit Certificate | Code: 60006 | 12 Credits
CIP (0413121000)
Effective Term: Spring 2020 (2203)

This is a College Credit Certificate in Early Childhood Education with a specialization in Early Childhood Inclusion. This
is an introductory CCC for care practitioners who need training in addition to their initial credentials, i.e., Florida
Child Care Professional Certificate (FCCPC) or the National Child Development Associate (N-CDA) credential in order
to provide high quality early childhood education for all children.

MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (12.00 Credits)

EEC 1752        Knowing and Understanding All Young Children                      (3 Credits)
EEC 1753        Observing and Assessing All Young Children                        (3 Credits)
EEC 1713        Helping All Young Children Become Independent Learners            (3 Credits)
EEC 1308        Classrooms for All Young Children                                 (3 Credits)
