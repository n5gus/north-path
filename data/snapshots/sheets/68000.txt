Automation
College Credit Certificate | 68000 | 15 Credits
CIP: (0615040601)
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

This certificate prepares students for initial employment with an occupational title as an Automation or Applied
Automation Specialist in various specialized areas. It can also provide supplemental training for persons previously
or currently employed in these occupations. These courses can be applied toward the Associate of Science in
Advanced Manufacturing.
