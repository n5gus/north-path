Business Specialist – General Business
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65100 | 12 Credits
CIP (0552020103)
Effective Term: Fall 2018 (2187)

The Business Specialist College Credit Certificate is the first in a series of three College Credit Certificate programs
designed to prepare students for employment in entry-level positions in the following areas:
accounting/budgeting, business management, finance, general business, human resources, international business,
marketing, and small business. Students may select one of the 6 options, but the certificate is awarded only once.
