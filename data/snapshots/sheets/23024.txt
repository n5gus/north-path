Clinical Laboratory Science
Associate in Science | Code: 23024 | 76 credits
CIP (1351100405)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Clinical Laboratory Sciences program prepares the graduate to work as part of the health care delivery team in a non-profit clinical
laboratory or research laboratory. Clinical practice is conducted in local health care facilities under the supervision of qualified registered
professional personnel. Graduates are eligible for Florida State Licensure and Registry with the American Society of Clinical Pathologists and
equivalent licensure.
