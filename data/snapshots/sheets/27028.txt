Computer Crime Investigation
Associate in Science | Code: 27028 | 60 credits
CIP (1743040300)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Associate in Science (AS) in Computer Crime Investigation will prepare graduates with the education and skills needed to fulfill
roles and positions in the Information Security industry. The coursework will include education and applied technical skills in the
criminal justice and information security fields.
