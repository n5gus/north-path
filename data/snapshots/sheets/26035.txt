Civil Engineering Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 26035 | 63 credits
CIP (1715020101)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Civil Engineering Technology program is designed for those students who wish immediate job placement prior to or after
graduation. This program also satisfies many of the civil engineering freshman and sophomore requirements for the Bachelor of
Engineering Technology degree offered by certain universities. Consult your Civil Engineering advisor prior to registration.
