Early Childhood Education - Administrator
College Credit Certificate | 60004 | 12 Credits
CIP (0419070906)
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

This is a College Credit Certificate with a specialization in Child Care Management. The purpose of this program is
to prepare students as early childhood education administrators with the knowledge and skills to effectively
manage a quality childcare program or to provide supplementary training for persons previously or currently
employed in these occupations. This CCC will allow its holder to apply for an Advanced Director's Credential or
continue their education.

MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (12.00 Credits)

EEC 2002          Operation of an Early Childhood Facility                              (3 Credits)
EEC 2520          Early Childhood Organization Leadership and Management                (3 Credits)
EEC 2523          Programming & Management for Early Childhood Administrators           (3 Credits)
EEC 2527          Legal & Financial Issue in Child Care                                 (3 Credits)
