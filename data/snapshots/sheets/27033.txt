Early Childhood Education – Administrator
Associate in Science | Code: 27033 | 60 credits
CIP (1413121004)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Early Childhood Education Associate in Science degree program provides the student with appropriate coursework in Administrator
Specialization. Students may complete the Advanced Director's Credential and/or College Credit Certificates as part of their Associate in
Science degree. This program is stackable towards the Bachelor of Science (BS) in Early Childhood Education.
