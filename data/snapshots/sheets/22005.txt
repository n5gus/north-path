Fashion Design
Associate in Science | Code: 22005 | 60 credits
CIP (1450040700)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Associate in Science degree in Fashion Design prepares students to be leaders in the global fashion industry. Fashion Design
courses ae intensive and real-world, focusing on the theoretical and practical, from the creative process and principles of design
to the use of state-of-the-industry technology and software. All CTE classes require department approval.
