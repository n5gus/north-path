College Credit Certificate| 68001| 12 Credits
CIP: ( )
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

This certificate prepares students for initial employment with an occupational title as a Quality Specialist or Lean
Specialist in various specialized areas. It also can provide supplemental training for persons previously or currently
employed in these occupations. These courses can be applied toward the Associate of Science in Advanced
Manufacturing.
