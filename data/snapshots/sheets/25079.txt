Cybersecurity
Associate in Science | Code: 25079 | 60 credits
CIP (1511100308)
Effective Term: Spring 2025 (2253)

The Cybersecurity program prepares students to fill a critical and growing need for cybersecurity personnel through hands-on
experience along with simulation training and group/team-based learning to simulate a professional work environment. The
program covers a wide range of topics including cybersecurity fundamentals, computer forensics and network security. Upon
completion of the program, the student will have learned to evaluate security trends, recognize best practices, and understand
IT security products and threats. Additionally, the curriculum is designed to aid students in preparing for many of the
certification exams in the field.
