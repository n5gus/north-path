Virtual and Augmented Reality Technologies
College Credit Certificate | Code: 66060 | 19 Credits
CIP (0550041118)
Effective Term: Fall 2023 (2237)

The program will teach students the fundamentals of Virtual and Augmented Reality. Students will learn basic concepts,
history and tools commonly used for stereoscopic image acquisition and immersive technologies. Students will also learn
the origins of Virtual Reality (VR) and its current role in the industry, its applications and opportunities and how to
generate and manipulate VR imagery.
