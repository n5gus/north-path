Culinary Arts Management Operations
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65060 | 18 Credits
CIP (1612050401)
Effective Term: Fall 2022 (2227)

The Culinary Arts Management Operations College Credit Certificate is designed to prepare students with an in-
depth study of food production, and a practical foundation in international cuisine for a successful career in the
culinary industry. Credits earned can be applied to an Associate in Arts degree or an Associate in Science degree in
Culinary Arts Management, which may be transferrable to upper division public institutions. *Students will be given
opportunity to take the Food Safety exam for ServSafe Florida State Certification (State mandated for food handlers
in Florida).
