Animation & Game Art
Associate in Science | Code: 25074 | 60 credits
CIP (1610030400)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Animation & Game Art is an intensive hands-on program in which students learn narrative structure along with technical skills
required in the animation industry. Students learn character design and animation, 3D modeling, storyboarding and environment
design. Graduates are prepared for entry level jobs as 3D artist and animators.
