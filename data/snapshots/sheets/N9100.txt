Nursing
Bachelor of Science | Code: N9100 | 125 credits
CIP (1105138012)
Effective Term: Spring Term 2026 (2263)

The Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) is designed for licensed RNs with an A.S. degree in Nursing from regionally accredited
programs who wish to attain the next level of education in order to provide professional nursing care in all clinical practice
settings around the world, or to be eligible for advanced nursing leadership, management, staff education and practice
positions, in a multicultural society.
