Internet of Things (IoT) Applications
College Credit Certificate | Code: 66057 | 24 Credits
CIP: (0511020110)
Effective Term: Spring 2026 (2263)

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a rapidly growing field converging with emerging technologies. This certificate program
prepares graduates to advance their careers by building skills in embedded programming, data analytics, cloud computing,
artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. Students learn to program connected devices, secure networks, and apply AI-driven
insights to IoT data. Through hands-on projects and industry-standard tools, the program equips students to design, deploy,
and manage smart, connected solutions across diverse technology sectors.
