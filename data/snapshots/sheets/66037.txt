Help Desk Support Technician
College Credit Certificate| Code: 66037 | 16 Credits
CIP: (0511010313)
Effective Term: Fall 2020 (2207)

The Help Desk Support Technician College Credit Certificate is designed to prepare students with the technical
knowledge and skills for employment as entry-level computer help desk and support technicians in commercial,
industrial and government institutions. Graduates are also prepared for CompTIA A+ and Network+ industry
certifications.
