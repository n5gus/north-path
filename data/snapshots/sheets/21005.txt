Landscape and Horticulture Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 21005 | 60 credits
CIP (1101060502)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The purpose of this program is to prepare students for employment in horticulture and landscape industries. The students will learn about
plant growth, nutrition and fertilization, plant classification and identification, propagation, pest control, pruning, maintenance and drainage.
Students will also gain business management and job skills.
