Aviation Maintenance Management
Associate in Science | Code: 26027 | 83 credits
CIP (1649010401)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Aviation Maintenance Management program provides the aviation professional with an extensive academic background to
complement their aviation technical background whether it is in Air Traffic Control, Aviation Maintenance, Flight Operations, Dispatch
Operations through verified FAA Certifications. Only those students holding an FAA A&P certificate may enroll in this degree program.
The program provides a solid general education combined with the flexibility for coursework covering a wide range of specific aviation
courses.
