Business Administration
Associate in Science | Code: 25051 | 60 credits
CIP (1552020102)
Effective Term: Spring 2026 (2263)

This program transfers to four-year institutions. See department for information. The Business Administration program trains
individuals to assume management or supervisory positions in business, industry, and government. It provides basic skills in a
broad range of business functions including accounting, computer usage, management and marketing. Successful completion of
this program earns the student entry into any university in the State University System as part of the A.S. to B.S. program.
