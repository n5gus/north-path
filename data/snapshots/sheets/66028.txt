Passenger Service Agent
College Credit Certificate |Code: 66028 | Credits
CIP:
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

The Passenger Service Agent College Credit Certificate program is designed to give students the skills required to
gain employment as a passenger service agent, including gate and ramp responsibilities. Students will be required
to do an internship with a commuter or major airline. Additional Information: Contact the Aviation Department at
(305) 237-5950 for more information and advisement.
