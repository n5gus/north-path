Information Technology Support
College Credit Certificate | Code: 66044 | 28 Credits
CIP: (0511010311)
Effective Term: Spring 2022 (2223)


The Information Technology Support College Credit Certificate is designed to provide an opportunity to establish a
basic foundation in IT roles. Training in this program includes computer repair and installation, troubleshooting
operating systems, understanding the foundations of networking technologies, and providing excellence in customer
service. Graduates are also prepared for CompTIA A+, Network+, and Project+ industry certifications.
