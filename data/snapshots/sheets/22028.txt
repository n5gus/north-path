Biotechnology – Bioinformatics
Associate in Science | Code: 22028 | 61 credits
CIP (1341010100)
Effective Term: Fall Term 2024 (2247)

The Biotechnology Program will expose students to a breadth of topics and emphasizes hands-on learning in a variety of
techniques and procedures necessary for employment in the bioscience industry. The Program includes modules designed to
enhance critical thinking and technical communication skills. It focuses on developing broad transferable skills and stresses
understanding and demonstration of laboratory/industry protocols and regulations, bio-safety and safe operating procedures,
ethical and environmental issues, product generation/formulation, quality control, validation, instrumentation, and computing.
