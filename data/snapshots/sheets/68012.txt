Commercial Transport Pilot
College Credit Certificate | Code: 68012 | 24 credits
CIP: (0649010202)
Effective Date:

The College Credit Certificate (CCC) in Commercial Transport Pilot indicates the student has successfully passed the
Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) exams to earn the Commercial Pilot Certificate and act as pilot-in-command
for compensation or hire, as well as carry persons or property for compensation or hire, as outlined in Federal
Aviation Regulations (FARs) Part 61.133.
