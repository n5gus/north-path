Sport Management
Associate in Science | Code: 28010 | 60 credits
CIP (1531050400)
Effective Term: Fall 2025 (2257)

The Associate in Science (AS) in Sport Management focuses on learning through practical, hands-on experiences, and bolsters the students’
learned knowledge with theory, industry insight gained from professionals with years of experience within sport. Students will experience
sporting events through the lens of a manager to better understand the roles and responsibilities that are necessary to operate sports at an
amateur and professional level. In addition, AS will help students understand the best practices of business, professional networking, problem-
solving, and project and event management. Graduates will be able to gain employment in various athletic, entertainment, and institutional
settings.
