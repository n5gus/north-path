Funeral Service
Associate in Science | Code: 23049 | 72 credits
CIP (1312030100)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

Students in the Funeral Service program are given a broad understanding of all phases of funeral home operations as well as the
public health responsibilities of the funeral director and embalmer. The Funeral Service degree program at Miami Dade College is
accredited by the American Board of Funeral Service Education (ABFSE), 992 Mantua Pike, Suite 108, Woodbury Heights, NJ 08097,
(816) 233-3747. Web: www.abfse.org. All students must register to take the International Conference of Funeral Service Examining
Boards, Inc. National Board Examination during their final semester. Students who plan Funeral Service licensure in other states
must contract the respective state board to determine licensing requirements and the student is responsible for complying with all
particular laws of that state. National Board Examination pass rates, graduation rates, and employment rates for this and other
ABFSE-accredited programs are available at www.abfse.org. To request a printed copy of this program’s rates, go to the Funeral
Service Education Office (Building 3142) or by telephone 305-237-1244.

Program Requirements :

 -      Students must complete all coursework with a “C” or better to complete the degree.

 -      Students must complete all general education and pre-entry coursework with a minimum of 3.0 GPA for program
        admission.

 -      Students who receive a total of three grades of ‘D’, ‘F’, or ‘W’ across both pre-entry and major courses will be subject to
        removal from the program.

 -      Students who completed any FSE course more than 3 years ago will be required to either take a competency course or
        retake the class.

 -      Students must maintain a 2.5 GPA while admitted to Associate in Science program or be withdrawn from the
        program.

 -      Students must be enrolled in at least 9 credit hours per major semester (Fall and Spring) in an academic year or they
        will be removed from the program.

 -      Students may take up to 12 credit hours of course requirements at another institution while admitted to the
        Associate in Science degree program.

 -      Students must be available to attend in-person and blended courses when available.

 -      Students are strongly encouraged to take SPC1017 – Fundamentals of Speech Communication prior to entry of the
        program.
