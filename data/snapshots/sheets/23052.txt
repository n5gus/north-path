Health Information Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 23052 | 70 credits
CIP (1351070700)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Health Information Technology program is designed to educate and prepare graduates with entry-level job skills.
The Associate in Science degree in Health Information Technology engages students in healthcare, business, and the
technology used in the industry.
