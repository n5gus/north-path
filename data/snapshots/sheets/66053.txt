Mechatronics
College Credit Certificate | Code: 66053 | 30 Credits
CIP: (0615000013)
Effective Date:

This program offers students instruction in maintenance techniques, computer aided drafting/design skills,
technical communications, maintenance and operation of various industrial components, quality control and
testing, material handling protocols, and proper usage of tools and instrumentation.
