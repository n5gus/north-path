Building Construction Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 26033 | 60 credits
CIP (1615100102)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Building Construction Technology program is designed to furnish technically trained personnel for the building construction
industry. The graduate may work with a contractor as part of the administrative team in such entry-level job positions as those
leading to estimators, job coordinators, or project managers. Technical jobs may also be available in the following areas: land and
project developers; technical sales for building materials, systems, and equipment; with local, state, and federal government
agencies; as well as various financial institutions.
