Respiratory Care
Associate in Science | Code: 23046 | 76 credits
CIP (1351090800)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Respiratory Care program prepares the successful graduates for employment in health agencies where they will work with
the physician and other professionals in treating patients with respiratory ailments or injuries affecting the respiratory function.
Emphasis will be placed on supervised clinical instruction and practice in local health care facilities. Completion of this two-year
accredited program enables the graduate to apply for entry into the Examination Process of the N.B.R.C.A grade of “C” or better
is required in each course.
