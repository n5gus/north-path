Digital Marketing Strategy
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65102 | 18 Credits
CIP (0252070103)
Effective Term: Fall 2019 (2197)

The Digital Marketing Strategy College Credit Certificate is designed to prepare students to design, implement,
manage and analyze digital marketing strategies and campaigns. Graduates will understand how to connect with
consumers using multiple digital platforms to create effective customer-focused promotional campaigns.


MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (18.00 Credits)

MAR 1011          Principles of Marketing                             (3 credits)
MAR 1720          Marketing in a Digital World                        (3 credits)
MAR 2101          Social Media Marketing                              (3 credits)
MAR 2704          Marketing Web Analytics                             (3 credits)
MAR 2703          Marketing Content, Branding and Strategy            (3 credits)
MAR 2952          Digital Marketing Capstone                          (3 credits)
