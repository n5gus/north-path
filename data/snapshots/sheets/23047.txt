Emergency Medical Services
Associate in Science | Code: 23047 | 73 credits
CIP (1351090402)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Associate in Science degree in Emergency Medical Services is designed according to national and state standards. Graduates will
perform as advanced practitioners and as leaders in the technical supervisory and managerial aspects of advanced emergency care.
Graduates will be prepared primarily for employment in agencies of advanced emergency care. Graduates will be prepared primarily
for employment in agencies providing pre-hospital emergency medical care and secondarily, for jobs in emergency and other acute
care areas of the hospital.
