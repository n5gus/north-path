Chef Apprentice
College Credit Certificate | 65059 | 12 Credits
CIP (0612050302)
Effective Term: Fall 2026 (2267)

The Chef Apprentice Certificate is designed to prepare students with a theoretical and practical foundation for a
successful career in the culinary industry. Credits earned can be applied to Associate in Science degree in Culinary
Arts Management, which may be transferrable to upper division public institutions. *Students will be given
opportunity to take the Food Safety exam for ServSafe Florida State Certification (State mandated for food
handlers in Florida).


MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (12.00 Credits)

FSS 1204L        Food Production 2                            (3 credits)       Corequisite: FSS1200
FSS 1202L        Food Production 1                            (3 credits)       Prerequisites: FSS1200, FSS1202C
