 CIP:
 Effective Date:

 The Human Services program with a specialization in Addiction Studies is designed to prepare students
 for employment as human services specialists, human services practitioners, chemical dependency
 practitioners, addiction specialists, mental health and social service practitioners, or to provide
 supplemental training for persons previously or currently employed in these occupations. The program is
 also designed to provide most of the general academic and addiction specific requirements of the
 Certification Board for Addiction Professionals of Florida.
