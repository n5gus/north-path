Air Cargo Management
College Credit Certificate |66030| 16 Credits
CIP:
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

The Air Cargo Management College Credit Certificate program is designed to give students the skills required to
gain employment as an air cargo agent. The program can be completed in one or two semesters with classes
offered during the day or evening hours. All of the credits earned can be applied towards an A.S. degree in Aviation
Administration. Contact the Aviation Department at 305-237-5950 for information and advisement.

MAJOR COURSE REQUIREMENTS (16.00 Credits)
