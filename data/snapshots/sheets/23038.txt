Diagnostic Medical Sonography
Associate in Science | Code: 23038 | 77 credits
CIP (1351091004)
Effective Term: Spring 2025 (2253)

The Diagnostic Medical Sonography program prepares the student to become a Diagnostic Medical Sonographer. The Diagnostic
Medical Sonographer provides patient services, using diagnostic ultrasound under the supervision of a doctor of medicine or
osteopathy who is responsible for the use and interpretation of ultrasound procedures. The sonographer assists the physician in
gathering sonographic data necessary to reach diagnostic decisions.
