Procurement Management
Advanced Technical Certificate | Code: C6003 | 18 Credits
CIP (0652020967)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

Supply chain management is an interdisciplinary field that emphasizes cross-functional integration of diverse business
functions, facilities, and activities and seeks to manage those activities to enhance a company’s competitive
advantage. The Advanced Technical Certificate (ATC) in Procurement Management is designed to designed for current
and pre-professionals interested in working in purchasing, project management, contracting, and supply chain
management.
