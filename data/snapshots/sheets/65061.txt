Business Industry Operations
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65061 | 9 Credits
CIP (0652020502)
Effective Term: Fall 2022 (2227)

The 9-credit-hour certificate program in Business Industry Operations, includes a series of courses that offer
students the technical knowledge and skills needed to prepare for further education and careers in a variety of
industries aligned to a student’s pathway.
