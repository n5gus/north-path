Film Production Fundamentals
College Credit Certificate | Code: 61001 | 24 Credits
CIP (0650060203)
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)


The Film Production Fundamentals College Credit Certificate (CCC) is designed to prepare students for entry-level
employment in the motion picture industry. Students will understand the fundamentals in the following skills:
lighting, grip, camera, audio recording, and editing.
