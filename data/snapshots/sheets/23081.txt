Heath Science
Associate in Science | Code: 23081 | 60 credits
CIP (1351070101)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Health Sciences program provides students an introduction to the healthcare field and an in-depth science background to
prepare them for a health-related career or a graduate professional health program or other graduate program.
