Music Business – Performance and Production
Associate in Science | Code: 25044 | 64 credits
CIP (1650091300)
Effective Term: Fall 2025 (2257)

The Associate of Science (A.S.) in Music Business - Performance and Production track offers a comprehensive program for students
interested in performing, producing, and composing music. This track is designed to develop a well-rounded understanding of both
musical artistry and the business aspects of the music and entertainment industries. Students in this program engage in coursework
that includes business essentials, music business practices, music production, performance training, and music theory. The curriculum
equips students with the skills needed to manage their own careers as performers and producers while building a strong foundation in
the professional and creative aspects of the industry.
