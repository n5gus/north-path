Surgical Technology
Associate in Science | Code: 23077 | 64 credits
CIP (1351090901)
Effective Term: Fall 2024 (2247)

The Associate in Science degree in Surgical Technology is designed to educate and prepare graduates with skills required for
entry-level employment as a member of the healthcare team. In addition, students will acquire extensive knowledge of legal and
ethical responsibilities, anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, microbiology, aseptic techniques, patient care procedures,
surgical technology procedures, patient safety, and use and care of equipment and supplies.
