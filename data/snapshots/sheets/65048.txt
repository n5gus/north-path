Rooms Division Management
College Credit Certificate | Code: 65048 | 30 Credits
CIP: (0252090402)
Effective Term: Fall 2017 (2177)

The Rooms Division Management College Credit Certificate program is designed to prepare students with a theoretical and
practical foundation for a successful career in the hotel sales and marketing industry. Students enrolled in this certificate are
prepared for positions such as Front Desk Manager, and Guest Relations Manager. Credits earned can be applied to an Associate in
Applied Science degree in Hospitality and Tourism Management.
