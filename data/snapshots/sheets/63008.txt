Paramedic
College Credit Certificate | Code: 63008 | 42 Credits
CIP (0351090405)
Effective Term: Fall 2008 (2087)

The Paramedic College Credit Certificate provides students with the advanced skills, knowledge, and clinical
experience required to provide safe and effective hospital and pre-hospital care to the sick
and injured. Satisfactory completion of the program will qualify the graduate to sit for the State and/or National
Paramedic Certification Examination. This program is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of Allied
Health Education Programs (www.caahep.org) upon the recommendation of the Committee on Accreditation of
Educational Programs for the Emergency Medical Services Professions (CoAEMSP) and approved by the State of
Florida, Department of Health and Rehabilitative Services
