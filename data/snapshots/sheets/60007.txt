Early Childhood Education - Child Development Early Intervention Specialization
College Credit Certificate | Code: 60007 | 36 Credits
CIP: (0419070904)
Effective Term: Spring 2020 (2203)

This is a College Credit Certificate (CCC) in Early Childhood Education (ECE) with a specialization in Child Development Early Intervention.
The purpose of this program is to prepare students as early childhood educators who can identify learning differences in young children and
meet their educational needs.
